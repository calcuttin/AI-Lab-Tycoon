Full-resolution PNGs and exact prompts are retained here. Optimized 320px JPEGs are in `public/images/characters/`. `CharacterPortrait` uses initials for the player founder, procedurally recruited employees and failed image loads. It does not assign one of these faces to unrelated employees.
